## Cognitive Sovereignty Clause
Cognition belongs to the individual.  

These frameworks may **never** be used to:  
- Profile, monitor, or extract private thought processes.  
- Manipulate or coerce individuals based on inferred cognitive states.  
- Transform bias signals into mechanisms of judgment or punishment.  

Cognitive privacy is recognized here as equal in status to bodily autonomy in medical ethics.  

Any use that violates this clause constitutes **automatic and irrevocable revocation** of license rights.
